Mackay runs on cane, coal and the coast. Since 2005, iScape has kept the offices behind them working.

What we look after

  • A helpdesk in Mackay

    Call and talk to a technician who knows your office, and who can be at your door the same day when a fix needs hands.

  • Backups that leave town

    Every night your files are copied to data centres well outside the cyclone zone, and we test that they restore, not just that the job ran.

  • Security to the Essential Eight

    Multi-factor sign-in, patching and email filtering set to the Australian Cyber Security Centre's Essential Eight, because the scam emails arrive with the storms.

  • Connections that hold

    NBN with automatic 4G or 5G failover, Starlink for the farm and the site office, and a UPS so a flicker doesn't take the server with it.

  1. First we walk your site.

    An hour at your office or shed: every computer, the modem, the backups, and who signs in to what.

  2. Then we write it down.

    A plain-English report: what's there, what's at risk, and what fixing it would cost.

  3. We fix what's urgent.

    Usually the backups, multi-factor sign-in and the tired router, in the first fortnight.

  4. Then we watch it.

    Monitoring, updates and the helpdesk from then on, for a fixed monthly fee.

About iScape

iScape was started in Mackay in 2005 by Peter Everitt and Stewart McKinnon, and joined with Veltech Computer Services in 2008.

We're who you call when the screen goes blank, the email stops, or the storm is two days out.

Questions we get asked

Do you only work in Mackay?

We're on site from Airlie Beach to Sarina and out to Moranbah, and we support offices anywhere remotely. Most problems are fixed without anyone driving.

What does it cost?

A fixed monthly fee, worked out per person and device after the site walk. You get the number in writing before anything starts.

We already have someone who does our IT.

Good. We can sit behind them: backups, security and the after-hours calls, so they're not the only one holding it together.

What happens when a cyclone watch is issued?

We check that last night's backups restore, finish any pending updates, and confirm everyone can sign in from home, before the office closes.

Can you look after Microsoft 365?

Yes: accounts, licences, email, Teams and SharePoint, set up so that the person who leaves doesn't take the files with them.

How fast do you answer?

Response times are written into your agreement, by how urgent the problem is. A down server isn't treated like a new mouse.
