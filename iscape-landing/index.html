Since 2005

We began writing software for Mackay's mining and sugar industries. Now we run the IT too.

Two directors founded iScape to build business software. The same team now designs, installs and looks after networks, servers and Microsoft 365 for businesses from a single server to several sites, with staff at our North Mackay head office and in Brisbane.

What we look after

The support desk

Email support@iscape.com.au and a ticket opens itself. Call 1800 775 800 when it's urgent. Our engineers fix it remotely or come to you.

Every ticket is tracked in Halo PSA and every system is documented in Hudu, so the next engineer already knows your setup.

Cyber security

Huntress managed detection and response, watched 24/7 by an Australian security operations centre. When something trips, we coordinate containment and help clean up.

Plus CyberAware phishing and safe-computing training for your staff, and a monthly review of your Microsoft 365 security settings.

Backup you've seen restore

Image-level Hornetsecurity backups of your servers, copied offsite to Wasabi storage in Australian data centres, encrypted in transit and at rest.

We restore a sample every month and run integrity checks every quarter, so the first real restore isn't the first test.

Monitoring and patching

NinjaOne watches your servers around the clock: availability, disks, memory, event logs, RAID health and backup jobs. Problems raise a ticket before your staff notice.

Windows Server, Office and third-party apps like Java and Adobe are patched for you, with annual firmware updates on HPE servers.

Microsoft 365 and domains

Business Premium set up properly: multi-factor sign-in, conditional access, Intune device management and data loss protection, plus new starters and leavers handled for you.

Your domain names renewed through Dreamscape and your DNS run on Cloudflare with DNSSEC, with every change approved by you first.

Software we write

Where we began. Custom applications in C#, .NET and SQL Server, reporting and business intelligence, telemetry and GIS mapping, and the Excel and Access tools your team already relies on.

application control, patching applications, Office macro settings, user application hardening, restricting admin privileges, patching operating systems, multi-factor authentication and regular backups.

Fair questions

Do we have to sign a long contract?

No. Our managed IT agreements can run month to month. Everything we provide, with its price and the Essential Eight controls it covers, is set out in plain language before you sign.

Where is our backup data kept?

Offsite in Wasabi's Australian data centres, separate from your premises and encrypted in transit and at rest. We test-restore a sample every month and check backup integrity every quarter.

Who's watching for attacks at 2 am?

Huntress's security operations centre in Australia, 24 hours a day. When it confirms a threat we review the alert, coordinate containment and help with clean-up through our service desk.

Will someone actually come onsite?

Yes. Most issues are fixed remotely because it's faster, but our engineers work from North Mackay and Brisbane and come to you when the job needs hands on hardware.

How is it priced?

A fixed monthly price for what we look after (servers, devices, users and backup storage), with hourly rates for projects. After-hours work is charged at 1.5 times the normal rate and emergencies at twice. We quote after a look at your setup.

Can you build software as well as fix computers?

It's how iScape started in 2005. We write custom applications, reports and integrations in C#, .NET and SQL Server, including telemetry and GIS mapping systems.
